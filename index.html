What this is

A directory, not an authority.

The carnivore conversation is scattered across a thousand videos, podcasts and forum threads. This site collects the entry points in one place — free, no account, no newsletter, no upsell.

Everything here is a position held by somebody, not a settled fact. Doctors in this space disagree with each other, and long-time carnivores disagree with the doctors. We link out to what they actually said so you can read the original and make up your own mind.

This isn't a balanced survey of nutrition science, and it doesn't pretend to be — the name tells you whose voices are in here. Mainstream medicine says different things about a lot of this. We're not hiding that; we're just not the site that covers it. Read what's here as one side of an argument you're free to go check.

Nothing on this site is medical advice.

Starter directory

Where the conversation is happening

A starting set — not a ranking, not an endorsement. Each of these people publishes publicly; the links go straight to them.

  • Dave Mac

    Interviewer · No Carb Life

    Records conversations with carnivores about their own experience — several a week, well over a thousand so far. The largest open archive of first-person accounts in this community, and the same channel the stories below point to.

    YouTube

  • Dr. Shawn Baker

    Orthopaedic surgeon · author

    One of the most visible advocates for meat-based eating. Long-form interviews and a community where people document their own experiments.

    YouTube Site

  • Dr. Ken Berry

    Family physician

    Makes the case for what he calls a "Proper Human Diet" and is openly critical of mainstream dietary guidelines. Short, practical explainers.

    YouTube Site

  • Ben Bikman

    Metabolic scientist · Brigham Young University

    Runs a university lab on insulin and metabolism and argues that insulin resistance sits underneath much of what gets called chronic disease. Wrote "Why We Get Sick". Explains mechanisms rather than asserting conclusions.

    YouTube Site

  • Dr. Elizabeth Bright

    Osteopath and naturopath · Italy

    An American osteopath and naturopath based in Italy who makes the case for high-fat, animal-based eating, with a particular focus on women, hormones and thyroid. Argues most people — women especially — eat far too little fat. Wrote "Good Fat is Good for Women". Direct and practical.

    YouTube Site

  • Coach Carnivore Cam

    Cameron Glover · health coach

    Works the fitness end of this — fat loss, training, and a steady run of recipes aimed squarely at the food people say they miss. Practical rather than theoretical, and the closest thing on this list to a cooking channel.

    YouTube

  • Carnivore Teacher Alpha

    Teacher · daily practice

    Short, frequent videos on the day-to-day of eating this way — which foods, what he thinks they do, and where he believes the common advice goes wrong. Wrote a book on it. Speaks with certainty, which is the draw for some viewers and the reservation for others.

    YouTube

  • Dr. Anthony Chaffee

    Medical doctor

    Argues that plants aren't required in a human diet. Deep, hours-long interviews if you want the full argument rather than the summary.

    YouTube

  • Judy Cho

    Board-certified holistic nutritionist

    Nutrition with Judy. The practical end of the spectrum — how people structure elimination-style carnivore eating, and what tends to go wrong.

    YouTube

  • Dr. Georgia Ede

    Psychiatrist · nutrition and mental health

    A psychiatrist who argues that what you eat shapes mental health as much as physical, and that diet belongs in the conversation about depression, anxiety and brain fog rather than medication alone. Wrote "Change Your Diet, Change Your Mind". The one voice here aimed at the brain rather than the body.

    YouTube Site

  • Dr. Sten Ekberg

    Holistic health educator · former Olympian

    A former Olympic decathlete turned one of the most-watched health explainers on YouTube — whiteboard breakdowns of insulin, fasting and metabolic health. Broad, beginner-friendly, keto-and-wellness more than carnivore-specific; a clear on-ramp to the "why".

    YouTube

  • Maria Emmerich

    Keto and carnivore cook · author

    One of the most prolific recipe writers in the low-carb world — the practical answer to "what do I actually cook". She leans keto rather than strict carnivore, so she is here for the food and not as a protocol, but the recipes-and-cooking end of this list is largely hers.

    YouTube Site

  • Dr. Gary Fettke

    Orthopaedic surgeon · Tasmania

    Came to nutrition through orthopaedic surgery, arguing that much of what he was operating on — diabetic amputations among it — traced back to diet. Makes the case for lower-carb, real-food eating through No Fructose.

    Site YouTube

  • Jason Fung

    Nephrologist · fasting & obesity

    A kidney specialist who became one of the most influential voices on fasting and type-2 diabetes through "The Obesity Code" and "The Diabetes Code". Fasting- and low-carb-focused rather than carnivore — the hormonal case for why calorie-cutting fails underpins much of it.

    YouTube

  • Kelly Hogan

    Long-term carnivore

    Fourteen-plus years eating this way. Interviews other people about what long-term zero-carb has actually been like for them, day to day.

    YouTube

  • Bart Kay

    Former academic · nutrition science

    Reads nutrition studies closely and argues the field's methods rarely support the conclusions drawn from them. Blunt and adversarial by style — which is the appeal for some viewers and the objection for others.

    YouTube

  • Dr. Robert Kiltz

    Fertility doctor · founder of CNY Fertility

    Runs a large fertility practice and argues for high-fat, animal-based eating with long gaps between meals — what he calls the Kiltz Method. Posts daily, and is the most relentlessly upbeat voice on this list.

    YouTube Site

  • Robert Lustig

    Professor emeritus of Pediatrics · UCSF

    Argues the problem is metabolic dysfunction rather than obesity itself, and that processed food and sugar are what drive it. Wrote "Metabolical" and "Fat Chance". Comes at this from public health and biochemistry — he is not a carnivore advocate, and is here as background rather than as a protocol.

    Site YouTube

  • Dr. Paul Mason

    Sports and exercise medicine physician

    Works through the primary literature in unusual detail and argues the mainstream reading of it doesn't hold up. Dense, citation-heavy talks — the closest thing in this list to sitting through a lecture.

    YouTube

  • Nick Norwitz

    Metabolism researcher · MD, PhD

    Oxford PhD in metabolism and Harvard-trained MD who runs experiments on his own bloodwork — the Lean Mass Hyper-Responder work on why lean, low-carb people can show very high LDL. Young, rigorous, one of the sharpest voices on cholesterol and keto metabolism.

    YouTube

  • Amber O'Hearn

    Long-term carnivore · researcher-writer

    Has eaten a nearly plant-free diet since 2009 — her own account — and is one of the most analytical voices in it: peer-reviewed papers on ketogenic and all-meat eating, conference talks, and a blog she has kept for over a decade. Closer to the "why it works" end of this list than the motivational one.

    Site YouTube

  • Dr. Philip Ovadia

    Cardiac surgeon · metabolic health

    A heart surgeon with more than three thousand operations behind him, arguing that most of what reaches his table is metabolic and was preventable. Wrote "Stay Off My Operating Table". Talks about insulin resistance and inflammation rather than cholesterol alone.

    YouTube Site

  • SHAPEFIXER

    Lifestyle and nutrition coach

    A lifelong athlete who put on weight in middle age, turned down heart surgery and a hip replacement, and went at it through diet instead — his own account. Now coaches, and spends much of his airtime pulling apart nutrition claims he thinks do not hold up.

    YouTube

  • Richard Smith

    Nutritionist · UK

    Went from clinically obese with a type 2 diagnosis to competing as a British and European men's physique champion — his own account, told at length. Now a UK nutritionist and Noakes Foundation ambassador who works through the biochemistry rather than selling the before-and-after.

    YouTube Site

  • Laura Spath

    Long-term carnivore · author

    Lost a hundred and twenty pounds on carnivore in 2018 — her own account — and has been unusually open about the years since, the stretch where the weight came back and she lost it again included. Co-wrote "The Complete Carnivore Diet for Beginners" with Judy Cho. The how-do-I-start end of the list.

    YouTube Site

  • Coach Stephen

    Coach · The UK Carnivore

    A coach with a physiology degree who spends his time on the people actually attempting this — sleep, training, bloodwork and the daily practical questions. Closer to the how-do-I-do-this end of the list than the debate end.

    YouTube Site

  • Lisa Wiedeman

    Optometric physician · long-term carnivore

    An optometrist who has eaten this way since 2009 and now coaches other people through it, under the name CarnivoreDoctor. Posts her own experience, her recipes and the practical detail of keeping it up over years — the long view rather than the first ninety days.

    YouTube Site
